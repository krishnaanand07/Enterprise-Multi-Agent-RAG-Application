SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC RAG ROADMAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Q3 2026, the Enterprise AI Systems Architecture Board initiated a comprehensive architectural overhaul of our autonomous research and engineering platforms. As large language models (LLMs) mature, enterprise architectures are shifting away from standalone monolithic prompts toward Multi-Agent Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) state machines and deterministic conversational retrieval pipelines.

1.1 Advanced RAG Architecture: The Switch to Hybrid Retrieval & Re-ranking
Previously, our primary document repository relied exclusively on zero-shot dense cosine vector retrieval using standalone vector store indexes. While computationally rapid (~8ms average query lookup), empirical evaluation via the RAGAS (Retrieval-Augmented Generation Assessment) benchmarking suite revealed a 14.2% hallucination rate when handling ambiguous financial abbreviations and multi-lingual technical telemetry.

To resolve this, the engineering team transitioned our standard extraction and indexing pipeline to a Hybrid Retrieval methodology. This dual-track indexing strategy combines:
  1. Sparse Lexical Matching (BM25): Ensures precise keyword adherence for exact product codes, uuid database keys, and proper nouns.
  2. Dense Semantic Embedding (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 / bge-small-en-v1.5): Captures underlying conceptual intent across extended paragraphs.

Following retrieval, top-K candidate chunks are evaluated through a Cross-Encoder Re-ranker (BAAI-bge-reranker-large), which compresses context recall latency while reducing enterprise factual hallucination rates to under 1.8%.

SECTION 4: SECURITY, COMPLIANCE, AND AUTOMATED NE-MO GUARDRAILS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To satisfy SOC2 Type II and GDPR regulatory mandates, the architecture integrates real-time Prompt Injection and Jailbreak defense via deterministic input firewalls.

4.1 PII Redaction and Compliance Enforcement
Any document uploaded to the ingest server undergoes automatic named entity recognition (NER) scrubbing before persistent vector storage. Personally Identifiable Information—including social security numbers, bank routing credentials, and proprietary encryption secrets—is systematically replaced with cryptographic cryptographic hash markers.

4.2 Observability Tracing & Telemetry (OpenTelemetry + Phoenix)
Every edge transition within our LangGraph supervisor engine emits structured OpenTelemetry traces. Engineers can observe step-by-step latency execution, token burn rates per sub-agent (SQL Agent, RAG Retriever, OCR Parser), and embedding cosine similarity distance distributions in real time through custom diagnostic UI cards.
